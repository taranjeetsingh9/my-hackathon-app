# PRD

## Problem
People do not need more productivity tools. They need a calmer place to slow down, name what is loud, and choose a next step that feels possible.

## Goal
Create a product that turns a messy mental state into one honest next action and offers future-self reflection through letters.

## Target user
- Busy professionals
- Students and creatives
- Anyone carrying a lot of mental load

## Core flows
1. User writes a mind dump.
2. User chooses available time and energy.
3. App recommends one small, realistic action.
4. User marks progress.
5. App saves check-in history and suggests a refined goal update.
6. User can view or generate monthly or milestone letters from future self.

## Success metrics
- User can complete full flow in under 5 minutes
- AI guidance is grounded in the user’s own text and history
- Letters feel personal and calm instead of jargon-heavy
- Product is demo-ready without requiring complex setup

## Non-goals
- Advanced scheduling
- Team collaboration
- AI therapy claims or medical overreach
- High-friction onboarding
