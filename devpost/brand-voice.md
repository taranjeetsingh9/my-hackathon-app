# Mybee Brand and Voice Direction

## Brand name
Mybee

## Core feeling
Mybee should feel like a quiet companion for busy, overwhelmed people. It should be warm, soft, personal, and not overly polished or clinical.

## Visual direction
- pastel palette
- gentle, airy spacing
- warm neutrals and soft magical tones
- softly rounded surfaces
- soft gradients, not harsh contrast
- texture and mood inspired by storybook magic rather than startup professionalism

## Recommended color palette
- lavender
- blush pink
- soft cream
- muted sage
- warm yellow
- dusty blue
- mint green

## Tone of AI responses
The AI should feel calm, reflective, and encouraging. It should speak in a way inspired by the themes of Ikigai: purpose, small steps, meaning, rhythm, and daily joy.

### Guidelines
- gentle, not bossy
- personal, not robotic
- affirming without being cheesy
- practical without being cold
- thoughtful and grounded in real life

### Avoid
- cold corporate language
- over-optimistic productivity slogans
- harsh urgency
- clinical task labels
- sterile dashboard aesthetics

## Letter tone
The daily and monthly letters should feel like they were written by a thoughtful future self. The mood should be safe, personal, and quietly magical.

The magical messenger theme should feel like a soft literary fantasy:
- owl
- raven
- phoenix
- fox
- bird or animal chosen by the user

This adds delight without making the product too playful or childish.

## Product identity
Mybee is not a productivity app. It is a calm companion for people who need one next step, one meaningful action, and one reassuring message from their future self.
