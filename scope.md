# Scope

## Product
Mydobby is a calming check-in and planning app for people who feel overloaded. It helps them write what is on their mind, choose how much time and energy they have, and get one small next step that feels realistic.

## In scope
- No-login onboarding flow
- Mind dump capture
- Time and energy selection
- AI-generated next-step plan
- Goal tracking and AI-refined goal suggestions
- Daily check-ins with saved history
- Monthly and milestone future-self letters
- Local demo data for testing when no active profile exists

## Out of scope
- Login, authentication, billing, or multi-user accounts
- Real-time collaborative editing
- Complex analytics dashboards
- Publishing infrastructure or deployment automation
- Extensive long-tail edge cases beyond the proof-of-concept

## Constraints
- Must work as a simple afternoon build
- Must feel warm, minimal, and emotionally safe
- Must be easy to explain in a 2-minute demo video
- Must run end-to-end with a lightweight local prototype and seed data
