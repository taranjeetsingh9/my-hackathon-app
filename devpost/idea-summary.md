# Product Idea Summary
## App name
Mybee

## Brand direction
Mybee is a calm, pastel, emotionally supportive app. It should feel warm, gentle, and a little magical, not clinical or overly corporate. The design should avoid harsh professional styling and instead feel like a soft daily companion.

The AI voice should be influenced by the themes of Ikigai: purpose, small meaningful progress, everyday joy, and thoughtful rhythm. It should sound reflective and grounded, not preachy or robotic.
## Core concept
An app for busy people who feel stuck. It turns a messy brain dump into one small, meaningful action, then sends a future-self letter based on what they actually did.

## Differentiator
This is not a generic task app. The key value is emotional momentum + future accountability.

The feature to protect most is the future-self letter:
- monthly letters with a magical messenger theme
- milestone letters that are more personal and grounded
- optional bird or animal messenger for delivery style

## Target user
Busy people who feel overwhelmed, scattered, or stuck and want help taking one meaningful next step instead of feeling pressure to fix everything at once.

## Primary experience
1. User writes or speaks what is on their mind.
2. User shares available time and energy.
3. The app helps choose one small action toward a meaningful goal.
4. The user completes, updates, or adjusts the step.
5. The app tracks real progress.
6. After enough progress, the app sends a letter from the user’s future self.

## Product narrative
The app should feel calming, supportive, and non-judgmental. It should reduce paralysis by focusing on the next small step instead of the whole life plan.

## MVP product story
The user does not need to manage tasks manually. They can describe what happened, and the AI can update the plan and progress based on what they say they did.

## Important product decisions
- Goal selection is optional and not required on day one.
- Goal choice happens after check-ins or after enough context is gathered.
- The user can keep the app in general support mode without committing to a formal goal.
- The AI should update progress based on user confirmation such as: “I did it,” “I finished,” or “I changed it.”
- Journal writing is optional, but useful for richer future letters.
- History tracking is required only for important progress and letter generation.

## Letter system
### Monthly letters
Atmospheric, magical, and immersive, with a messenger such as an owl, raven, phoenix, or another bird/animal chosen by the user. This should feel like a storybook or movie-style delivery.

### Milestone letters
More grounded, personal, and reflective. Based on the user’s real actions, conversations, and progress. This should feel like a letter from their future self, not a generic summary.

### Messenger theme
The user can choose the letter carrier, which adds personality and delight to the experience.

## MVP feature list
### Must-have
- Mind dump input (text or voice)
- Optional goal selection after context is built
- Time and energy input
- One small next-step recommendation
- Progress updates from user confirmation
- Future-self letter generation
- Minimal history log
- Optional journal entry support

### Nice-to-have later
- analytics dashboards
- deep journaling system
- advanced habit tracking
- calendar integrations
- social features
- premium personalization features

## MVP scope guardrails
Do not build a generic task manager.
Do not prioritize advanced productivity analytics.
Do not overcomplicate planning.
Keep the app focused on small actions, reflection, and future-self letters.

## Summary sentence
A busy person tells the app what is on their mind, the app suggests one achievable next step based on their time and energy, and after real progress it sends them a letter from their future self.
